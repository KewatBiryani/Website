OUR STORY

The Story Behind
Kewat’s Biryani

From a passion for authentic flavours to a place made for every biryani lover.

Founded by Niraj Kewat, Kewat’s Biryani was created with a simple vision — to serve delicious, aromatic and satisfying biryani that people can truly enjoy.

What started with a love for the perfect blend of fragrant rice, rich spices and perfectly cooked ingredients has grown into a restaurant dedicated to making biryani the star of every meal.

At Kewat’s Biryani, every plate is prepared with attention to flavour, quality and consistency. From the first aroma to the last bite, our aim is to give you an experience that feels authentic, comforting and memorable.

“Kewat’s Biryani — where every biryani tells a story.”

Niraj Kewat
OWNER, KEWAT’S BIRYANI
